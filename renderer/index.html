Madden NFL 26 Offline Franchise Merge — co-op

Brings one player's played game into the shared save — result, player stats (season & career), standings, and box score. Pick the three files, preview what will come in, then merge. Your files are read-only: the merge only writes the new output file you name; nothing else on your computer is touched.

What's merged: game result, player season stats (league leaders), standings (W/L record), career totals, per-player game logs, and the team/player box score. Madden NFL 26 offline franchise only. (College Football 27 and Madden 27 support are planned for after those games ship.)
